WHY IT BEGAN?

Pharmacy is not limited to learning what is already known. It is also about questioning, exploring and creating what comes next.

Pharmentia began with the idea of creating a space where students could move beyond passive learning and actively engage with research, scientific thinking and innovation.

A place where a question could become a research idea, an idea could become a concept, and a concept could become something worth sharing.

FROM CURIOSITY → TO CONCEPT → TO INNOVATION

Empowering Next-Gen Pharmacists

Pharmentia is the official Research & Development club of AIKTC School of Pharmacy. We foster a community of passionate scholars dedicated to drug discovery, novel dosage formulations, and scientific publications.

Through access to research guidance, peer-review circles, and industry guest lectures, we bridge the gap between pharmacy curricula and modern pharmaceutical industry standards.

WHAT IS RESEARCH?

Research is a systematic process of asking a question, investigating it scientifically and generating evidence-based conclusions.

  • • Novel drug-delivery systems
  • • Pharmaceutical quality-control studies
